# Parallel Frames

The visual idea is convergence: moving images and sound travel on separate paths, then become one saved object. Repeated marks refer quietly to samples and frames. Shapes carry the meaning; words are unnecessary in the small promotional tile.

Use the established rose icon against a deep blue field. White provides one clear destination. Carefully calibrated contrast and crisp, antialiased edges should keep the composition legible even at half size.

Spacing is structural. Two source forms share an axis and feed a single destination through restrained connecting lines. The geometry should feel meticulously crafted, with equal optical weight rather than decorative complexity.

The final refinement is subtraction. Keep a small palette, preserve generous internal space, and remove any mark that does not explain the relationship. The craftsmanship should be evident in alignment, proportion, and clarity rather than additional ornament.
